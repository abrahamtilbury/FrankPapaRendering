Solid Wall Plastering

Internal float and white set, and external rendering on brick and block. Over forty years experience across Perth.

Services

Solid wall plastering only. We work directly on masonry — no gyprock, no ceiling work.

01

Internal Float & White Set

Traditional two-coat solid plastering on brick or block. A grey float coat is applied first to create a strong, even base. A hard white set coat is then applied on top to produce a smooth, dense, paint-ready surface.

Float coat White set Paint ready
02

External Rendering

Sand finish and traditional external renders applied directly to solid masonry. Suitable for new work, renovations, and refreshing weathered or painted brick surfaces.

Sand finish External render Weather protection

What we don’t do

We specialise only in solid wall plastering. We do not install or finish gyprock, plasterboard, or ceilings.

If your project requires solid plastering on brick or block — internal or external — we can help.
